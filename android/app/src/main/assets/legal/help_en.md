# Help

## Which tools work offline?
Create PDF, Images to PDF, Merge, Split, Compress, Organise pages, Annotate, Sign, PDF to images, Protect, Remove password, Scan, basic on-device Word (DOCX) to PDF and PDF to Word, on-device text recognition and the editor all work without internet.

Cloud (best quality) Word to PDF and PDF to Word, cloud compression, cloud text recognition, handwriting recognition in the cloud and all AI features need internet.

## Is my original file changed?
No. Every tool writes a new file. Your original is never modified or deleted.

## Why is my compressed PDF not smaller?
Compression mainly shrinks photos and scans inside a PDF. Text-only or already optimised PDFs may not get smaller. The app shows the real sizes and lets you keep the original.

## How accurate is handwriting recognition?
It depends on the writing and the photo. On-device recognition is designed for printed text. Cloud handwriting recognition is much better but can still make mistakes, so always review the text before exporting.

## Is the drawn signature legally valid?
The app places an image of your drawn signature. It is not a certified digital signature and does not prove identity.

## What happens to files I send to the cloud?
They are sent over an encrypted connection, processed, and deleted from the server — inputs immediately after processing and results as soon as the app downloads them, or within one hour at most. They are never used to train AI models.

## Where are my documents stored?
In private storage inside the app. Use "Save to device" or "Share" to put a copy elsewhere. Uninstalling the app removes them.
