## Who we are
One-Minute Docs Ltd. ("we"). Registered address and contact details: [to be completed].

## What the app does with your documents
- Most tools process files only on your device. Nothing leaves the device for these tools.
- Cloud features (Word to PDF, cloud PDF to Word, cloud text recognition, handwriting recognition, AI features) upload only the file or text you choose, after showing what will be uploaded.
- Uploaded inputs are deleted when processing ends. Results are deleted when you download them or after at most 60 minutes.
- AI text and handwriting images are sent to our AI provider solely to produce your result. We do not use your content to train models.

## Data we collect
- No account, name, email or phone number is required.
- An anonymous app identifier (random, not linked to you) is used to keep your cloud jobs separate from other users' jobs and to apply fair-use limits.
- Technical logs (time, operation type, status, file size) are kept for up to 30 days for reliability. Logs never contain document contents, file names or passwords.

## Permissions
- Camera: only when you start a scan.
- Internet: only used for cloud features.
- No access to your other files: you choose files with the system picker.

## Your choices
Use offline tools only, decline cloud consent, delete documents in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
