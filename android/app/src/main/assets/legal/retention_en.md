# Data Retention — DRAFT

| Data | Where | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| Your documents and drafts | On your device | Until you delete them or uninstall |
| Temporary processing files | On your device | Deleted after use; leftovers cleaned within 12 hours |
| Uploaded inputs (cloud) | Our server | Deleted when processing ends |
| Cloud results | Our server | Deleted on download, or after 60 minutes |
| Job records (no content) | Our server | 7 days |
| AI usage records (sizes, status — no content) | Our server | 30 days |
| Audit events (deletion/expiry, no content) | Our server | 90 days |

Content sent to the AI provider is subject to that provider's API data terms; it is not used for training.
