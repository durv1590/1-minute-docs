# Google Play store listing (draft)

**App name:** One-Minute Docs
**Short description (≤80):** Scan, create, convert and organise PDFs — plus handwriting and AI drafts. Free.

**Full description:**
One-Minute Docs turns paper, photos and ideas into clean documents in moments.

• PDF tools — create PDFs from text or templates, merge, split, compress (with real before/after sizes), reorder, rotate and delete pages, annotate, sign, protect with a password and convert pages to images. Most tools work offline.
• Scanner — capture multi-page documents with automatic edge detection, perspective correction and clean-up filters.
• Handwritten Notes to PDF — photograph your notes, recognise and correct the text, and export a scanned PDF, an editable PDF, a Word document or AI-organised study notes.
• AI Document Creator — draft letters, leave applications, resumes, reports, minutes, notices and more in English or Hindi, then edit and export.
• Conversion — PDF to Word and Word to PDF.
• English and Hindi interface.

Your files stay on your device unless you choose a cloud feature; the app tells you exactly what will be uploaded first, and uploaded files are deleted after processing. No account needed. No ads.

AI results and recognised text can contain mistakes — please review before use. Drawn signatures are images, not certified digital signatures.

## Data safety (preparation)
- Data collected: none linked to the user. App activity/diagnostics: operation type and status only on the server (no content).
- Files and docs: processed ephemerally on the server only when the user starts a cloud feature; not shared with third parties except the AI provider for AI features; encrypted in transit; deleted after processing.
- Users can delete data: yes (in-app delete; server copies auto-delete within 60 minutes).
