## What the app does with your documents
- Most tools process files only on your device, including on-device text recognition. Nothing leaves the device for these tools.
- Cloud features (cloud Word to PDF, cloud PDF to Word, cloud compression, cloud text recognition and searchable PDF, cloud handwriting recognition, AI features) upload only the file, page photos or text you choose. Before each upload the app shows what will be uploaded, unless you have turned on "Don't ask before cloud processing" in Settings (you can turn it off at any time).
- Uploaded files are deleted from our server when processing ends. If a job is never processed, its files are deleted when it expires (about 75 minutes after upload with the current server settings). Results are deleted when the app downloads them, or at most 60 minutes after processing finishes.
- Text you send to AI features is passed to our AI provider and the result is returned to the app; it is not stored on our server.
- AI text and photos of handwritten notes are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude API), solely to produce your result. Cloud conversion, compression and text recognition run on our own server with open-source tools and are not sent to the AI provider. We do not use your content to train models.
- Our server and its temporary file storage run on infrastructure provided by [HOSTING / CLOUD STORAGE PROVIDER].

## Data we collect
- No account, name, email or phone number is required.
- An anonymous app identifier (random, issued by our server, not linked to you) is used to keep your cloud jobs separate from other users' jobs and to apply fair-use limits. It is stored encrypted on your device and replaced with a new one at least every 30 days.
- Your IP address is used only to limit how often new app identifiers can be requested. It is held in a temporary counter for about one hour and is not written to our database or application logs.
- Cloud job records (operation, settings, status, times, error code, and each file's name, type, size and checksum) are kept until about 7 days after the job ends. They never contain document contents or passwords.
- AI usage records (task type, model, text length, token counts, status, response time) are kept for 30 days. They never contain the text you sent or the AI output.
- Audit records of cancellations, deletions and expiries (with job and app identifiers only) are kept for 90 days.
- Server logs (time, request path, status, job identifier) never contain document contents, file names, passwords or your app token. They are kept for [LOG RETENTION PERIOD].
- The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Permissions
- Camera: only when you start a scan. Importing from the gallery works without it.
- Internet: only used for cloud and AI features.
- No access to your other files: you choose files, and any folder to save into, with the system picker.

## Storage on your device
- Documents are kept in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
- Drafts that are not changed for 30 days are deleted automatically. Temporary processing files are deleted after use; leftovers are removed by a periodic cleanup.

## Your choices
Use offline tools only, decline cloud consent (or keep "Don't ask before cloud processing" turned off), delete documents in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
